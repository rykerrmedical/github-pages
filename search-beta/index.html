Search beta

Beta testing for our new search feature! Give 'er a whirl and let us know what you think. She peruses an index of all the content we've made to find answers to questions or to pull content related to keywords. The system falls back to things we've referenced and a few trusted sources when we don't have anything specific in our content library. That said, she won't spit out a concise answer (i.e., she isn't an AI chat bot), rather she points you to the right place to look. She's still a work in progress, but we're getting there!
