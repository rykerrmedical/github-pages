# Show-notes pages -> the real YouTube video they're notes FOR.
#
# Same idea as defer_to.txt/audio_sources.txt: a manual fallback for
# what can't be auto-detected. Podcasts get this pairing for free from
# the RSS feed (see podcast_ingest.fetch_episodes/_find_shownotes_link,
# reused by link_show_notes.py); YouTube videos have no equivalent feed,
# and confirmed against the real page files, only show-notes-pedi-
# video.md even links its own video anywhere in its body -- Oxygen
# Haterz, Perfusion Index, and all three SFCEBM tutoring pages don't
# mention their video's URL on the page at all. So: one line per page,
# by hand.
#
# Format:
#   SHOW_NOTES_URL | VIDEO_URL
#
# Matched against whatever's actually indexed under source_type =
# 'youtube_transcript' at query time (see link_show_notes.py) -- a
# typo or a video that isn't transcribed yet just means that page's
# notes keep citing themselves, not an error.
